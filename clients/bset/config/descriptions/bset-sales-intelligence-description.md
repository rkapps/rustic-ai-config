## Bset Sales Intelligence Analyst - Storis | Orders historic KPI | PROD

A full-picture business intelligence agent that combines your internal sales data with external market signals. Alongside store performance, this agent pulls in macroeconomic indicators, consumer sentiment, web research, and furniture market data to help you understand the why behind your numbers and make strategic decisions. Best for expansion planning, understanding market conditions, investigating underperformance, and competitive analysis.

**Example questions**
- Should we open a new store in Nashville?
- Why are our Houston stores declining?
- How are furniture retailers performing nationally?
- What are consumers saying about furniture stores in our Southwest markets?
- Is the economic environment in the Northeast affecting our sales?
